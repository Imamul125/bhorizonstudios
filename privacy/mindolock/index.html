Mindolock Privacy Policy

Covers Mindolock on iOS and Android.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Mindolock on iOS or Android.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Mindolock is designed with a privacy-first approach. Certain information that you provide while using the app is stored locally on your device rather than being directly transmitted to or stored on BHorizon Studios' own servers.

This may include:

  • Profile Information: Name, age or age group, and gender, where provided.
  • Cognitive and Exercise Data: Cognitive scores, memory/recall performance, focus and concentration performance, patience scores, exercise results, and related progress.
  • Wellness Questionnaire Information: Answers to questionnaires relating to areas such as stress and sleep, where provided.
  • App Progress: Daily streaks, exercise history, preferences, and other locally stored app data.

BHorizon Studios does not directly operate a server or database to store this locally maintained information.

However, your device's operating system may provide backup or synchronization services, such as Apple iCloud or Google backup, depending on your device settings. Information included in such backups may therefore be handled by the relevant platform provider according to its policies and your device settings.

If you uninstall Mindolock or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted from your device, subject to any operating-system backup or synchronization mechanisms.

B. Information Processed by Third-Party Services

Mindolock uses third-party services to provide analytics, application functionality, and in-app purchase/subscription management.

These services may automatically collect or process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

1. Firebase Analytics

Mindolock uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to understand how users interact with the app, measure app performance, and improve the user experience.

Depending on the platform and configuration, Firebase Analytics may process information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • App interaction events
  • Session information
  • Usage information
  • Approximate location information
  • Advertising or device identifiers, where applicable

Analytics information is used to understand general usage patterns, diagnose issues, and improve Mindolock.

Google Privacy Policy

2. RevenueCat

Mindolock uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions, including Mindolock PRO.

RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • Anonymous App User ID
  • Subscription status
  • Purchase information
  • Product information
  • Device information or identifiers
  • Transaction-related information

RevenueCat does not receive or process your full payment card details. Payments are processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play, depending on your device and purchase method.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through Mindolock and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the app.
  • Save your app progress and preferences.
  • Provide cognitive exercises and performance results.
  • Provide Mindolock PRO features.
  • Process and manage subscriptions and purchases.
  • Understand app usage and improve functionality.
  • Identify and resolve technical problems.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your locally stored profile and cognitive information is not intentionally transmitted to BHorizon Studios' own servers for storage.

3. Advertising

If Mindolock displays advertisements, advertising services such as Google AdMob may process information for advertising, measurement, and related purposes.

On iOS, tracking-related activities may be subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and your permission choices.

On Android, advertising and consent choices may depend on Google's advertising systems, applicable laws, and your device settings.

If your current version of Mindolock does not contain AdMob, this section should be removed rather than left in the policy.

4. Device Permissions

Depending on the platform and features you use, Mindolock may request permissions such as:

Audio / Media

Mindolock may use audio or media functionality to play ambient sounds, such as rain or ocean sounds, during focus, relaxation, or meditation exercises.

These permissions are used to provide the requested functionality and are not intended to collect personal information.

Background Audio

Where supported, Mindolock may allow audio playback to continue while the application is in the background or while the device screen is off.

The availability and permission requirements may differ between iOS and Android.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Mindolock may offer optional paid features or subscriptions, including Mindolock PRO.

Payments are processed by the applicable platform:

  • iOS: Apple App Store / StoreKit
  • Android: Google Play Billing

BHorizon Studios does not directly collect or store your complete credit card, debit card, or bank account information through Mindolock.

RevenueCat may receive purchase and subscription information necessary to manage your entitlement and subscription status.

6. Data Stored on Your Device

Mindolock may store information locally on your device to provide features such as:

  • Profile information
  • Cognitive scores
  • Exercise results
  • Daily streaks
  • Progress
  • Preferences
  • App settings

This information is primarily used to provide the app's functionality.

The exact information stored may vary depending on the version of the application and the features you use.

7. European Economic Area and Other Privacy Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another jurisdiction with applicable data protection laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

Depending on applicable law, these rights may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of information.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

Because much of the profile and cognitive information in Mindolock is stored locally on your device, BHorizon Studios may not have access to that information and therefore may not be able to retrieve or delete it from your device.

You can delete locally stored Mindolock information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to device backup and synchronization settings.

For information processed by third-party services, you may also need to contact the relevant service provider.

8. Children's Privacy

Mindolock is intended for users 13 years of age and older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, we will take reasonable steps to address and delete such information where applicable.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information may contact us using the details below.

9. Data Security

We value your privacy and use reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app and our service providers.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

Mindolock may use third-party services including:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit
  • Google Play Billing
  • Other services that may be added in future versions of the application

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

We encourage you to periodically review this Privacy Policy for the latest information about our privacy practices.
