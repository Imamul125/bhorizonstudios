Games Privacy Policy

Covers Arrow Escape: Time Trap and Last Brick on iOS and Android.

BHorizon Studios built this game as a free-to-play app. This SERVICE is provided by BHorizon Studios and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed on Android or iOS.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, postal address, or phone number.

However, third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect certain information necessary for advertising, analytics, notifications, game services, and app performance.

The information collected may vary depending on the platform, device settings, permissions, and services available on Android and iOS.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

These services include:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Firebase Analytics — analytics and app usage measurement
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
  • Google Play Games Services — leaderboards and achievements on Android

On iOS, Apple services and Apple's platform technologies may also process information associated with app purchases, notifications, advertising permissions, or other platform functionality where applicable.

These services may collect information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Usage data
  • App interaction data
  • Notification tokens
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Game-related information, where Google Play Games Services is used

For more information about Google's privacy practices:

Google Privacy Policy

Ads

This app displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

AdMob may use device information, advertising identifiers, and other information to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

On Android, advertising personalization may depend on Google's advertising settings and applicable consent requirements.

On iOS, advertising and tracking may be subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and the user's choices regarding tracking permissions.

Where required, the app may request consent before using information for personalized advertising.

Users can manage applicable advertising and privacy settings through their device settings and the privacy controls provided within the app.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect certain information to help us understand app usage and improve the app experience.

This may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Country or approximate location derived from IP address
  • Session duration
  • App usage behavior
  • App interactions
  • App version

Analytics information is used to understand how the game is used, identify technical issues, and improve the app.

The exact information collected may differ between Android and iOS depending on the platform and SDK configuration.

Push Notifications

This app may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send notifications such as:

  • Game updates
  • Announcements
  • Reminders
  • Event notifications

FCM may generate a unique device registration token to deliver notifications to your device.

On iOS, notification delivery also relies on Apple's push notification infrastructure.

Users can disable notifications through their device settings.

Google Play Games Services

On supported Android versions, this app uses Google Play Games Services to provide features such as leaderboards and achievements.

When you sign in to Google Play Games, Google may collect and process information such as your player ID, profile name, achievements, leaderboard scores, and gameplay activity in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

This information is used to provide game-related social and competitive features.

Google Play Games Services is not used for the iOS version of the app.

In-App Purchases

The app may offer optional in-app purchases.

On Android, purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

On iOS, purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

We do not directly receive or store your complete payment card or banking information. Payment information is handled by Google or Apple according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

European Economic Area (EEA) Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Where required, we request appropriate consent before processing information for personalized advertising or other purposes requiring consent.

You may accept or decline personalized advertising where applicable and manage available privacy and advertising preferences through the app or your device settings.

Vibration / Haptic Feedback

This app may use vibration or haptic feedback to enhance the user experience.

This feature does not collect or store personal information.

Log Data and Crash Information

In case of an error or crash, third-party services may collect Log Data or diagnostic information.

This may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • App configuration
  • Date and time of use
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information

This information is used to identify and resolve technical problems and improve app stability.

Data Stored on Device

The app may store certain game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress and settings.

If Google Play Games Services is used and you are signed in, certain game-related information such as achievements and leaderboard records may also be associated with your Google Play Games account.

The availability and handling of game progress may differ between Android and iOS.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

Information controlled by third-party services may be retained according to their respective policies.

You may uninstall the app at any time to stop its use on your device. Data stored by third-party services, such as Google Play Games records, may need to be managed through the relevant third-party account or service.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information where applicable.

Security

We value your trust and use reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Platform-Specific Privacy

This Game is available on both Android and iOS.

Some features and third-party services may differ between the two platforms. For example, Google Play Games Services is available on Android, while iOS uses Apple's platform services for functions such as App Store purchases and push notification delivery.

The information processed may therefore vary depending on your device, operating system, permissions, consent choices, and the features you use.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Contact Us

When you contact us through the in-app support feature, information such as your device model, operating system, and app version may be automatically included in your email to help us assist you.

This information is used only for customer support.
