Kill Code Privacy Policy

Covers Kill Code on iOS and Android.

Kill Code ("the game") is made by BHorizon Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Player ID. When you open the game you are signed in automatically as a guest. Our service provider, Unity, creates an anonymous player ID and stores an installation identifier on your device so you stay the same player.
  • Player name. The name you choose in the game. If you sign in with Apple or Google Play Games, the game may start from the first name or gamer name they share, and that name is saved with your account. Other players can see your name in matches, friends lists and leaderboards.
  • Game progress. Your level, XP, rank, coins, diamonds, unlocked items, loadout and leaderboard points. These are saved to the cloud under your player ID.
  • Linked sign-in (optional). You can link an Apple, Google or Google Play Games account. We receive an identifier for that account from the sign-in provider. We don't receive your password. If you sign in with Google, the sign-in goes through Unity Player Accounts, which stores your email address to create that account.
  • Purchases. When you buy diamonds, our server checks the proof of purchase from Apple or Google, and keeps a record of that purchase and your player ID so it pays out only once. We don't receive your card or bank details.
  • Friends. If you use the friends feature, we store your friends, friend requests, blocked players and online status.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Your phone gets a notification token, which we save with your player ID so friends' requests, new friends and room invites can reach you, along with occasional game news. Reminders (daily reward, streak, missions, season) are set up by the game on your phone and don't send any data. You can turn each kind off in Settings > Game > Notifications, or turn all notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Reports. If you report a player, we store the reported player's name, the reason, your player ID and the date.
  • Connection data. Online matches, voice chat and downloads connect through our service providers. Your IP address and connection details are processed to provide these features. Unity may also use your IP address to work out your approximate location, and it collects crash data and basic usage data to keep its services running.

Voice chat and the microphone

Kill Code has optional voice chat in friends' rooms and online matches. Your microphone stays muted until you tap the mic button, and the game asks for permission the first time. While you are unmuted, your voice is sent live to the others in your room or on your team through Epic Online Services. We don't record or store voice chat. Epic Online Services also creates a device-based ID for voice chat, without an Epic account.

You can mute yourself at any time, and mute other players during a match.

Play stats and crash reports (only if you agree)

The game asks before collecting these. If you tap Allow, it uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to send:

  • How you play: matches started and finished, mode, map, results, kills, level, season tier, daily rewards, items bought with coins or diamonds, and friend requests and invites sent.
  • Device and app details: phone model, operating system, game version, language and approximate country.
  • Crash reports: what the game was doing when it crashed.

These are linked to your player ID, not your name or email. We use them only to fix bugs and improve the game. There's no advertising ID and no tracking across other apps. You can turn this off any time in Settings > About > Share Play Stats. Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days and play stats for up to 14 months.

How we use information

We use this information only to run the game: signing you in, saving your progress, matching you with other players, running online matches and voice chat, delivering purchases, showing leaderboards and friends, handling reports, providing support, and, only if you agree, fixing crashes and improving the game using play stats.

We don't sell your information. The game has no ads, doesn't use an advertising ID, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

These providers process information for us under their own privacy terms:

  • Unity Gaming Services (sign-in, cloud saves, multiplayer, voice lobby matching, friends, leaderboards, game settings): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Epic Online Services (voice chat): https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy
  • Cloudflare (game content downloads): https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google (Google sign-in, Google Play Games and Google Play purchases on Android): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (push notifications; play stats and crash reports only if you agree): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

In-app purchases

The game offers optional diamond packs. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android, under their own terms.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your settings and a copy of your progress on your device. It may use vibration, which doesn't collect any information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your player data for as long as your account exists.

To delete your account in the game: open Settings, then Account, and tap Delete Account. This deletes your player account, your name, progress, diamonds, items and friends list straight away, and it can't be undone. Your name is also removed from the leaderboards, where the score stays without a name. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also ask Apple to end that link. If that doesn't work, the game shows you how to remove it in your iPhone's Settings.

You can also email bhorizonstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Kill Code - delete my account". We'll reply within 30 days.

We keep a record of completed purchases (a purchase ID and the old player ID) so that a purchase can't be paid out twice, and reports for as long as we need them to handle abuse. Uninstalling the game doesn't delete data saved in the cloud.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a player from the pause menu during an online match or from your friends list, and block a friend from your friends list. We review reports and may remove offensive names or restrict accounts.

Children's privacy

Kill Code isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area and the UK, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it's used. To ask, email us at the address below. If you're in the EEA or UK: we use your information to provide the game you asked for, and we collect play stats and crash reports only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > About.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. Emails sent through the game's support option also include your device model, operating system, game version and player ID to help us assist you.
